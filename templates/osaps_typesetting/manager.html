    About this plugin

    OS-APS Typesetting replaces Janeway's built-in Typesetting stage with one built around the OS-APS publishing suite. Typesetters download the manuscript, convert and correct it in OS-APS, then upload the exported PDF or HTML back here as galleys.

    Add it to a journal's workflow from the workflow settings, then manage articles from Articles in OS-APS Typesetting.
